About

Meet Skyrox.

I'm Skyrox, also known online as skygamm4. I make 3D models with a focus on creating assets that actually look good when they're dropped into a real project.

Most of my work revolves around modeling, shaping silhouettes, detailing assets and building pieces that feel like they belong in a finished game or scene.

Blender is my main tool, but the goal is always the final result rather than the software itself.
